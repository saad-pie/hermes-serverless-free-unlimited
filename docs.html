NotebookLM Video & Workspace Auth

Call the literal notebooklm model for automated video and audio overview generation.

1. NotebookLM Video Generation cURL Request

curl -s https://antigravity-seven-delta.vercel.app/v1/videos/generations \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer antigravity-free" \
  -d '{
    "model": "notebooklm",
    "prompt": "Cinematic explanation video based on research notes",
    "Theme": "Cyberpunk Neon"
  }'

2. Google Account Authentication & Workspace Integration

Antigravity supports Google Workspace OAuth integration to securely link Google accounts and ingest documents from Google Drive and Google Docs into your notebook generation pipelines.
